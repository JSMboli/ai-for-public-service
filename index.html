Course philosophy

Not AI theatre. A leadership framework for decisions that must survive scrutiny.

The course deliberately moves beyond tools and demos. It asks where AI should be used, what evidence leaders should demand, and how to preserve service quality, accountability and public trust.

01

Public value first

Start with a visible service problem: waiting time, backlog, repetitive work, poor access or avoidable cost. Technology follows the mandate — not the other way round.

02

Humans stay accountable

AI can inform, triage, draft and accelerate. Final responsibility remains with people, supported by clear review, escalation and appeal routes.

03

Trust is a design requirement

Explainability, auditability, fairness, privacy, security and monitoring are built into the operating model before a service goes live.

Working outputs

Participants leave with artefacts they can use — not just notes.

The applied workshops progressively convert a strategic problem into a defensible pilot plan.

01

Opportunity audit

A prioritised view of bottlenecks, candidate use cases, impact, effort, risk and first data/governance gaps.

02

Future-state workflow

A practical map of where AI augments work, where humans review, what evidence is logged and how exceptions move.

03

Governance & risk pack

A checklist covering explainability, bias, privacy, security, human review, challenge routes and operational monitoring.

04

90-day roadmap

A defined pilot, accountable owners, governance gates, success measures, learning loops and a stop/iterate/scale decision.

Days 1–30

Diagnose & design

  • Choose one high-value problem and service user.
  • Confirm sponsor, owner and cross-functional team.
  • Complete data, privacy, security and ethics checks.
  • Write the pilot charter and success metrics.
Days 31–60

Pilot & learn

  • Run a restricted sandbox or proof of concept.
  • Review outputs, logs, exceptions and feedback.
  • Track quality, bias and operational impact.
  • Document fixes, issues and training needs.
Days 61–90

Decide & prepare to scale

  • Stop, iterate or expand based on evidence.
  • Build scale-up recommendation and budget logic.
  • Update governance, communication and training.
  • Prepare the leadership briefing and next roadmap.

Frequently asked

A flagship course, with room to tailor the delivery.

The three-day structure is the full executive pathway. Bespoke briefings and consultancy engagements can focus on selected themes.

Who is the programme designed for?

Directors, senior civil servants, policy leaders, transformation teams and other decision-makers responsible for service delivery, strategy, digital change, governance or AI adoption.

Can the course be adapted to a specific organisation?

Yes. Use cases, workshop prompts, data-readiness questions, governance examples and the 90-day roadmap can be aligned to the organisation’s services, operating environment and priority problems.

Is this a technical coding course?

No. It is designed for leadership decision-making. Technical concepts are explained clearly, but the emphasis is on opportunity selection, operating models, governance, procurement, implementation and measurable public value.

Can you deliver shorter executive sessions?

The content can be repackaged into executive briefings, half-day or full-day workshops, or a focused consultancy engagement around a specific AI problem or pilot.

What happens after the training?

The capstone creates a 90-day pilot roadmap. A follow-on engagement can focus on readiness assessment, pilot design, governance, vendor due diligence, evaluation or leadership support.
